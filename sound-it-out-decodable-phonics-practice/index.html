Good questions

Why are there no pictures beside the words?

A picture of a dog next to “dog” lets a child guess from the picture. Here your child reads first and draws the story afterwards.

Which phonics order does it follow?

It starts with s a t p i n m d and adds a few sounds at each step: g o c k, then ck e u r, then h b f ff l ll ss, then j v w x, then y z zz qu. Find the last step where your child knows every sound and start there.
