How to use them at home

  1. Read the word list together first.
  2. Your child finds each word in the grid, letter by letter, and circles it.
  3. Read each word again after it is found.
  4. The friend at the bottom of each page gives a tip. On page 1: “check off the box when you find a word.”

Who these pages are for

Ages 5 to 7 · Kindergarten and 1st grade. Easy CVC word search puzzles with pictures, Reading Path Level 5. They practice finding short words letter by letter, and suit a child who can sound out three-letter words.
