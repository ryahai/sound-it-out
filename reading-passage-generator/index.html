Your worksheet:

Watch: how the free worksheet makers workA short lesson. Three steps, and every maker works the same way.

How to make your worksheet

  1. Choose “Type my own passage” and type or paste it. Add your questions, one on each line.
  2. Or choose “Find a ready story”, pick the reading level and type a topic.
  3. Choose the text size and how many lines each answer gets.
  4. Print. To keep a copy, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer.

Questions a beginner can answer

The questions that come with the library stories ask who, what and where, and every answer can be found in the words of the story. Ask your child to point to the words that give the answer.

Your own passage

Paste any text you have the right to use: a paragraph from a class book, a note you wrote, a fact page. Each new line in the box starts a new paragraph on the sheet.

Nothing is made up on the spot

The page prints what you type or a story from the library. It does not write passages or questions.

Good questions

How do I make a reading comprehension worksheet?

Type or paste a passage, type your questions one on each line, and press Print. It is free and there is no account.

Does it write the passage for me?

No. You type it, or you pick one of the checked stories in the library. Nothing is invented by the page.

How many questions can I add?

Up to six. Each one gets one, two or three lines for the answer.

Is what I type stored?

No. The sheet is made on your own device. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved.
