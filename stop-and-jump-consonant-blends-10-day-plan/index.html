What this is not

It is not a promise: some children hear every sound in a blend within a week, others need the ten days twice. It covers consonant blends only. Pairs such as sh, ch and th make one new sound; they are in Ship and Chip. Dropping a sound in a blend is not a sign of dyslexia on its own; if your child is seven or older, or reading is very hard in other ways too, talk to the teacher as well. There is no app and no screen time. A child who cannot read cat and hop yet starts with the blending plan, and the free check shows that. You will need a printer and scissors.

Questions

What do I need besides the pages?

A printer, a pencil, crayons, and scissors for the six blend cards. Nothing else, and no screen for your child.

What is a consonant blend?

Two or three consonants side by side, where each one keeps its own sound: st in stop, fl in flag, nd in hand. Your child already knows every sound. What is new is saying them one right after the other.

Why does my child read stop as top?

Said fast, two sounds side by side run together, so the ear catches only one, and the eyes skip a letter. It is very common. A finger under every letter, and a sound for every letter, fixes the habit.

Should I teach blends at the end or at the start first?

At the end. Blends at the end, like hand and lamp, are easier to hear, so this plan starts there. Then blends at the start, one family a day: with s, with l, and with r.

How old should my child be?

Most children who need this are five to seven. Your child should already read short words like cat, hop and sit, even slowly. The free check shows you whether that step is steady.

How long does a blends day take?

About five minutes: two minutes on the first job with you, then your child works with the sound track and reads three blend words to you at the end.

What if my child still drops a sound?

Go back one day and do the pairs again: top, then stop. Put a finger under every letter. The day 5 check tells you whether to go on or repeat days 2 to 4.

What comes after the ten days?

You can stop there. If you want daily practice to keep going, The One Sheet gives your child one sheet a day, made from what you tell us. It is a separate plan, $9.99 a month, and you can cancel any time.

Does anything record my child?

No. Nothing in this pack watches, listens to or records your child. You are the one who listens.

How do I get the videos and sound tracks?

The “start here” page of the book has a code to scan with your phone, and a short link. They open in your browser. There is nothing to install.

What if it is not right for us?

Write to hello@sounditoutreading.com within 30 days of buying and we will refund you. You do not need to give a reason, and you keep the pages.
