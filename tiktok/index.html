Sound It Out: send videos to TikTok

This page is for the Sound It Out team. It connects the Sound It Out TikTok account so our own finished videos can be sent to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

  1. Click the button and sign in with the Sound It Out TikTok account.
  2. TikTok asks to share the basic profile and to let us upload drafts. Click Allow.
  3. Each finished video then arrives in the TikTok inbox as a draft. The account owner reviews it, adds the caption and posts it.
