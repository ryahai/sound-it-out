What this is not

It is not a promise. Some children drop the habit in a week. Some need the ten days twice. It is not a test for dyslexia, and guessing at words on its own does not mean a child has it. If your child is seven or older, or finds reading very hard in other ways too, tell the teacher as well. It is not an app, and your child does not need a screen. It covers short words of two and three sounds. If your child cannot yet join sounds into a word, the blending plan comes first, and the free check tells you so. You need a printer and scissors.

Questions

Do I need a printer?

Yes. The pages are made to be printed once, on plain paper. The black-and-white copy uses very little ink.

Why does my child guess words when reading?

Because it is quick, and in a book with a picture for every line it often works. Some children were even taught to look at the first letter, look at the picture, and guess. It is a habit, and it is common at five, six and seven. It is not a lack of effort.

Is my child reading or memorizing?

Here is a quick way to see. Take three words from a book your child “reads” well, and write them on paper in a new order. If your child cannot read them there, the book is known by heart. That is a good memory. It is not yet reading. The free check does this for you, and day 8 of the plan is built for it.

How old should my child be?

Most children who need this are five to seven. Your child should already know most of the letter sounds, and be able to join three sounds into a word, even slowly.

School tells my child to look at the picture. Will this clash?

Pictures are fine for enjoying a story. This plan only changes the order: the letters first, the picture after. You can tell the teacher what you are doing at home.

How much of my time does it take?

About two minutes at the start of each day’s page, and a minute at the end when your child reads three things to you. Day 1 and day 10 are checks of about four minutes each.

What if my child cannot do a day?

Stop, and do the same page again tomorrow. Slow is the whole idea: a child who guesses is going too fast. The halfway check on day 5 tells you whether to go on or go back.

Does anything record my child?

No. Nothing in this pack watches, listens to or records your child. You are the one who listens.

How do I get the videos and sound tracks?

The “start here” page of the book has a code to scan with your phone, and a short link. They open in your browser. There is nothing to install.
