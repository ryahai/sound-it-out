The most common mistake: Saying the word when a guess comes
Not like thisYour child says cat for cap. You tell them: that says cap.
Like thisYou do not say the word. You say the same five words, then you wait.

What to say: “Look again. Touch every letter.”

Day 1

Find the Habit

About four minutes. Listen and mark. 1:24 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Words alone, words with a trick picture, then mixed-up words.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Read what you can. It is fine to go slowly.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child fits one line in the box on your page. Stop at the first one. That line names the habit and the days to do twice.

If the check was hard to do

If there are few circles but your child says the sounds one by one and the word does not come, stop here. That is blending. Do From Sounds to Words first. If your child got tired, stop and do part C tomorrow. Do not teach on the check.

Day 2

Touch Every Letter

A finger under each letter, left to right. 0:58 min

  • A finger under each letter, left to right.
  • Say each sound. Then slide, and say the word.
  • Read first. The picture comes after.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Look again. Touch every letter. Start on the first dot.”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child read at least two of jet, web and sun to you at the end, touching every letter.
