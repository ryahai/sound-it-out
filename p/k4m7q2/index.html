Welcome: what to expect

What this plan is, what is inside, and how to use it. 2:36 min

    The most common mistake: A stop after every sound
    Not like thiss ... a ... t, with a stop after each sound. The word never comes.
    Like thissssaaat, then sat. The first sound is held until the next begins.

    What to say: “Keep your voice on. Hold sss until your finger gets to a, then slide to the end.”
