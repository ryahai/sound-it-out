The most common mistake: Saying the word when the e is missed
Not like thisYour child says cap for cape. You tell them: that says cape.
Like thisYou do not say the word. You say the same words, then you wait.

What to say: “Look at the end. Is there an e?”

Hear the short and the long sounds

Tap to hear a sound. They come in twos: first the short sound of a letter, then its long sound. So a is followed by ai, e by ee, i by ie, o by oa and u by ue.
