The most common mistake: Spelling the word for your child
Not like thisYou say the letters or point to the slip, and your child copies.
Like thisYou wait. Your child reads the word back and finds the slip.

What to say: “Read it back. Does it say the word you wanted?”

Day 1

Find the Sticking Point

About four minutes. Listen and mark. 1:13 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Sounds heard, letters written, then eight words.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Write what you hear. It is fine to stop and think.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child wrote 7 or 8 of the 8 words in part C: start at day 8. Any other result: go on to day 2 tomorrow and do every day.

If the check was hard to do

If the check felt long, stop after part A and do parts B and C tomorrow. Do not teach in between. If part A has 4 or fewer, do every day in order and stay on days 2, 3 and 4 until the tapping is sure.
