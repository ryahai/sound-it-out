Day 1

Find the Starting Point

About three minutes. Listen and mark. 1:05 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Words alone, words in sentences, then six words to sound out.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Read the ones you know. It is fine to stop and think.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child sounded out 4 or more of the six words in part C: start day 2 tomorrow. If A is 14 or more and B is 7 or 8, do days 8 and 9 only.

If the check was hard to do

C has 3 or fewer: the trouble is sounding out, so do the blending plan first. Your page gives the link. A has 5 or fewer: do every day in order and give each page two days. If the check felt long, stop after part A and finish tomorrow.
