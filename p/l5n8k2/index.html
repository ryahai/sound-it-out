Day 1

Which Letters Are Known?

About four minutes. Point and mark. 0:34 min

  • A check, not a lesson.
  • Big letters, then small letters.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Say the ones you know. It is fine to skip.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child fits one line in the box on your page. Stop at the first one. That line tells you where to start.

If the check was hard to do

If your child got tired, stop and do the small letters tomorrow. A sound instead of a name is fine to hear, but mark only the names. Do not teach on the check.
