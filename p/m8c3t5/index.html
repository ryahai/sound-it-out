The most common mistake: Counting for your child
Not like thisOne thing is counted twice. You count them again for your child.
Like thisYou do not count for your child. You say two words, then wait.

What to say: “Touch and move. Touch each one once, and move it to the side.”

Day 2

Touch and Count

The three rules of counting. 0:57 min

  • Real things first: three, then five.
  • Touch each one once, and move it aside.
  • The last number tells how many.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Touch and move. What was the last number you said? That is how many.”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child counted 3 balls, 5 socks and 4 cars for you at the end, touching each one once, and said how many.
