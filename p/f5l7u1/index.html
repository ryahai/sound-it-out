The most common mistake: Moving on after one read
Not like thisIt comes out word by word: The. cat. sat. You go to the next one.
Like thisThe same sentence again: The cat sat on a mat. Now it is like talking.

What to say: “Read that one once more. Now tell it to me, like talking.”

Day 1

Find Where the Reading Sticks

About three minutes. Listen and mark. 1:22 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Ten words, four sentences, one long sentence.
  • No timer. Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Read it your own way. It is fine to stop and think.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child fits one line in the box on your page. Stop at the first one. That line names the days that matter most.

If the check was hard to do

If A has 3 or more Xs, stop here. Sounding out is not sure yet. Do the blending plan first, From Sounds to Words. If A has 6 or fewer check marks, do day 2 and day 3 twice each before you start day 4.

Day 2

Words All at Once

Sound it in your head. Say it once. 0:52 min

  • Sound it out in your head, lips closed.
  • Then say the whole word once.
  • Job two is with the sound track.
For your child: press play and step away

The voice tells your child what to do, waits, then says each word so they can check it themselves. Nothing listens to your child.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Touch the dots with your lips closed. Say the sounds in your head. Now say the whole word once.”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child said at least two of hen, pig and bus to you all at once, without sounding out loud.

Day 3

Again, and Easier

Six little words. Then the same ten words. 0:53 min

  • Six little words: tell them, do not sound them out.
  • The same ten words, three rounds, new order.
  • No timer. Never say faster.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Sound it out in your head, lips closed. Then say the word once. The little words I will tell you.”

Ready for day 4 when: Your child said at least two of ran, the and has to you all at once at the end.

Day 4

Scoop the Words

Two or three words, in one breath. 0:45 min

  • A curved line under two or three words.
  • You read the scoop. Your child says it after you.
  • Then your child matches scoops to pictures alone.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Hear me say it: the big dog. One scoop, one breath. Slide your finger and say it after me.”

Ready for day 5 when: Your child read three scoops to you at the end, each in one breath, without a stop between the words.

Day 5

The Halfway Check

Eight words and two sentences. 0:45 min

  • Eight words and two sentences. Do not help.
  • Eight or more of ten: go on.
  • Fewer: do days 2, 3 and 4 again.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Take your time. Sound it out in your head if you need to.”

Ready for day 6 when: Your child got 8 or more of 10 on the check. Go on to day 6 tomorrow.

Day 6

The Sentence Pyramid

Each line adds one word. 0:46 min

  • Each line adds one word.
  • Each time, your child goes back to the start.
  • By the last line, the first words just come.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Go back to the start of the line. You know those first words. Now add the new one.”

Ready for day 7 when: Your child read one whole sentence to you from the last line of a pyramid, right and without a stop.

Work it out. Make it smooth. Make it talk. 0:59 min

Day 8

Ask and Tell

The mark at the end tells the voice what to do. 0:42 min

  • A period: the voice goes down and stops.
  • A question mark: the voice goes up.
  • You read it, then your child says it after you.
For your child: press play and step away

The voice tells your child what to do, waits, then says each word so they can check it themselves. Nothing listens to your child.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Look at the mark at the end. Hear me ask it. Now you ask me, like you really want to know.”

Ready for day 9 when: Your child asked you one question with the voice going up, and told you one answer with the voice going down.
