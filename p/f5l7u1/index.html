Day 1

Find Where the Reading Sticks

About three minutes. Listen and mark. 1:22 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Ten words, four sentences, one long sentence.
  • No timer. Keep the page for day 10.
Day 2

Words All at Once

Sound it in your head. Say it once. 0:52 min

  • Sound it out in your head, lips closed.
  • Then say the whole word once.
  • Job two is with the sound track.
For your child: press play and step away

The voice tells your child what to do, waits, then says each word so they can check it themselves. Nothing listens to your child.

Day 3

Again, and Easier

Six little words. Then the same ten words. 0:53 min

  • Six little words: tell them, do not sound them out.
  • The same ten words, three rounds, new order.
  • No timer. Never say faster.
Day 4

Scoop the Words

Two or three words, in one breath. 0:45 min

  • A curved line under two or three words.
  • You read the scoop. Your child says it after you.
  • Then your child matches scoops to pictures alone.
Day 5

The Halfway Check

Eight words and two sentences. 0:45 min

  • Eight words and two sentences. Do not help.
  • Eight or more of ten: go on.
  • Fewer: do days 2, 3 and 4 again.
Day 6

The Sentence Pyramid

Each line adds one word. 0:46 min

  • Each line adds one word.
  • Each time, your child goes back to the start.
  • By the last line, the first words just come.
Day 7

Read It Three Times

Work it out. Make it smooth. Make it talk. 0:59 min

  • Read 1: work it out.
  • Read 2: make it smooth, after you.
  • Read 3: make it sound like talking.
For your child: press play and step away

The voice tells your child what to do, waits, then says each word so they can check it themselves. Nothing listens to your child.

Day 8

Ask and Tell

The mark at the end tells the voice what to do. 0:42 min

  • A period: the voice goes down and stops.
  • A question mark: the voice goes up.
  • You read it, then your child says it after you.
For your child: press play and step away

The voice tells your child what to do, waits, then says each word so they can check it themselves. Nothing listens to your child.

Day 9

A Story, Today and Tomorrow

The second reading is the smooth one. 0:42 min

  • Eight story words first, each all at once.
  • You read two lines. Your child says them after you.
  • Your child reads the story alone, and again tomorrow.
Day 10

The Final Check

About three minutes. Do not help. 0:46 min

  • First the story once more, as a warm-up.
  • Then the check. No help, and no timer.
  • The four new sentences matter most.
