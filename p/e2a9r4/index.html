The most common mistake: Saying the word for your child
Not like thisYour child is stuck, so you say it: “It is map.”
Like thisYou say the three sounds again, closer together, then wait.

What to say: “Listen again. I will say them closer together. m, a, p. What word is it?”

Day 1

Find the Sticking Point

About four minutes. Listen and mark. 1:24 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Starts the same, rhymes, then push together and pull apart.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “It is fine to stop and think. I will ask it one more time. Then you point.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child got A 3 or 4 and C 5 or 6: this plan is too easy, go to the blending plan. Any other result: start day 2 tomorrow.

If the check was hard to do

Do not repeat the check. Start day 2 tomorrow. A was 2 or fewer: do days 2 and 3 twice. A fine, B 2 or fewer: do day 6 twice. A and B fine, C 4 or fewer: do days 7 and 8 twice. Stop at the first line that fits.

Day 2

Starts the Same

Two words. Do they start the same? 1:01 min

  • Name every picture first.
  • Say two words and hold the start of each.
  • Your child circles the one that starts the same.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Listen to how they start. Sun. Sock. Now sun. Map. Which one starts like sun?”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child circled the right picture in at least three of the four boxes in job 2.

The Last Sound

The last thing your mouth does. 0:48 min
