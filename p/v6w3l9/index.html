Day 10

The Final Check

About four minutes. Do not help. 0:46 min

  • The final check. Do not help.
  • Every word is one your child has never seen.
  • Your page says what the result means and what to do next.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “These are new words. Take your time.”

The ten days are done when: Your child did the check without help. Read the box on your page and do what the first line that fits says. Type your numbers into the report at the bottom of this page.

Not there yet

Do the check on a good day, not when your child is tired. Do not help: it is a check, and the box on your page tells you what comes next.

Hear the sounds of the teams

Tap to hear a sound: the long sound of a (ay), the long sound of e (ea), the long sound of o (ow in snow), then ar, or, er (also ir and ur), oi (also oy) and ou (also ow in cow).

Your child’s report

After day 10, type in the numbers you wrote on your two check pages. You will see where your child started, where they are now, and what comes next.
