Day 1

Find the Trouble

About three minutes. Watch the pencil. 1:14 min

  • A check, not a lesson: watch and mark.
  • Your eyes are on the pencil, not on the paper.
  • Keep both pages for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Write it the way you write today. Any way is fine. We will look again in ten days.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child got A 3 or 4, B 9 or 10 and C 8 or more: start at day 9. Any other result: start day 2 tomorrow.

If the check was hard to do

Read the last box on your page from the top and stop at the first line that fits. A 2 or fewer: take two days on day 2. B 8 or fewer: every day in order, saying each path. B fine, C 7 or fewer: ask tall, small, or tail before each row.

Day 2

Get Ready to Write

Sit, pinch, paper. 1:22 min

  • No letters today.
  • Sit, pinch, paper: three things before the pencil moves.
  • Then four moves that make every letter.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Let us check three things. Feet flat. Pinch just above the cut wood. Helper hand on the paper.”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child showed you all three with no reminder: feet flat with arms on the table, the pinch, and the paper held still.
