Day 1

Find Where It Gets Lost

About five minutes. Listen and mark. 1:38 min

  • A check, not a lesson: listen and mark.
  • Words, then sentences, then two short stories.
  • Keep the page for day 10.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “It is fine to go slowly. Read it once more, then tell me what you can.”

What today’s check tells you: Your child got 6 or more of 8 in part A: start day 2 tomorrow. If no line in the last box of your page fits, do day 9, then the day 10 check.

If the check was hard to do

Read the last box on your page from the top and stop at the first line that fits. A 5 or fewer: a different plan first. B 2 or fewer: do days 2, 3 and 4 twice. B fine, the story your child read 0 or 1: do the day 5 game and day 9 twice.

Day 2

See It, Then Draw It

Make the picture in your head. Then draw it. 0:56 min

  • Your child reads the sentence two times.
  • Eyes closed: make the picture in your head.
  • Then draw it. Any drawing is fine.

If your child gets stuck today, say: “Read it again. What do you see? Who is in your picture? Where is it?”

Ready for day 3 when: Your child drew who and where for both job 2 sentences: a hen on a bus, and a dog going up a hill with the sun up.
