Free Decodable Story Game

📚 Read a Story

Five sentences about one person, one sentence at a time. Real reading, with every word decodable.

Play now, free

Choose the sounds your child knows:

Level 2Level 3Level 4Level 5Level 6Level 7

How to play

  1. The story appears one sentence at a time.
  2. Your child reads each sentence.
  3. At the end, your child tells the story back in their own words.

What it teaches

Reading several sentences in a row and remembering what they were about.

Which level?

Each level adds a few letter-sounds. Level 2 uses s, a, t, p, i, n, m and d. Higher levels add more. Every word in the game uses only the sounds of the level you choose. See the sounds in each level, or answer a few questions to find your child’s level.

Good to know

  • It runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.
  • No sign-up and no ads. Stars and progress are kept on your own device.
  • A grown-up sits with the child. The game cannot hear your child read, so you decide what counts as read.
