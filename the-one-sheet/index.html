How a day goes

One sheet a day. You say how it went, and the next sheet is made from what you said.

  1. PrintOpen your family page. Today’s sheet is waiting.
  2. Sit togetherA few minutes of your time at the start.
  3. Say how it wentEasy, Just right or Hard, and a note in your own words.
  4. Your sheet is madeOn the spot, with three lines that tell you what is on it and why.

If the same trouble comes up twice, the next sheet takes it a different way. When a sheet goes well, your child goes on from where they were.

Good to know

Every sheet has to pass the same strict rules before it reaches you.

Only words your child knows

Every word and sentence comes from what your child has already learned. Nothing new is slipped in.

Checked before it is sent

If a sheet does not pass every rule, it is never sent, and you get one that did.

Paper, not a screen

Your child works with a pencil. The family page is for you, for one minute a day.

Nothing watches your child

No camera and no microphone. You are the eyes. We keep a nickname and the reading record, and nothing else.

Questions

What do I need?

A printer, a pencil for your child, and about five minutes. There is nothing to install.

How is a sheet made for my child?

You say how the sheet went and what you saw. The Sheet Maker builds the next sheet around exactly that, out of words and sentences your child has already learned, and it is ready in seconds. When a day goes well, the next sheet brings the next new thing on the road. The only exception is the first two steps of the road, the listening games that come before any letters: there are no words to build from yet, so the sheet that fits what you said is chosen for you.

What can I write in the note?

Plain words. For example: the letters your child mixed up, the words that were missed, a sound that gave trouble, that your child guessed, or that they said the sounds but not the word. If the note is about something else, your choice of Easy, Just right or Hard still decides the next sheet.

What if my child already reads a bit?

Then the starting check places your child further along the road. The sheets begin there. Nothing before it is repeated.

How long does a sheet take?

A few minutes of your time at the start. Children work at different speeds, so we do not put a number on your child’s part.

How do I cancel?

Any time. Open your orders page at Polar, the shop that handles our payments, and cancel there.

Is my child’s information safe?

The family page stores a nickname and the reading record. Nothing else. Nothing watches, listens to or records your child.

Is there math and grammar too?

Yes. Math and grammar are their own plans, made the same way, at $9.99 a month each. Or take all three together for $14.95 a month. One family page holds them all.
