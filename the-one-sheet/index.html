Questions

What do I need?

A printer and about five minutes. A pencil for your child. There is nothing to install.

What age is it for?

About 4 to 8, wherever the starting check places your child.

What if my child already reads a bit?

Then the starting check places your child further along the road. The sheets begin there. Nothing before it is repeated.

How long does a sheet take?

A few minutes of your time at the start. Children work at different speeds, so we do not put a number on your child’s part.

What if a sheet is too hard?

Say so. Choose Hard. The next sheet goes back to an easier one, and then returns to the hard one.

How do I cancel?

Any time. Open your orders page at Polar, the shop that handles our payments, and cancel there. Or write to hello@sounditoutreading.com and we will stop it for you.

Is my child’s information safe?

The family page stores a nickname and the reading record. Nothing else. Nothing watches, listens to or records your child.

Why is it not free?

The free packs on this site are free, and they are paper. This is a different thing: a page that keeps your child’s place, takes in what you said, and picks the next sheet, every day. That takes work to build and to keep running, and the monthly price pays for it.

Does it teach math or handwriting?

No. It is for reading. Each sheet has a Write box, where your child traces or writes words from that sheet.
