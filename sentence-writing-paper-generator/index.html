Sentence writing paper generator

Lines to write on and a checklist for a good sentence

Print writing paper that reminds a beginner what a sentence needs. Under a small picture box is a checklist to mark: a capital letter, finger spaces, an end mark. Then come primary lines to the end of the page.

Pick a design

Now: . Hundreds of designs to choose from.

Choose each part yourself

To keep a copy, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. What you type stays on your device: it is not sent to us and not saved.

Your worksheet:

How to make your worksheet

  1. Keep the checklist on. Choose a small picture box, a big one, or none.
  2. Choose the size of the lines.
  3. Add a writing prompt if you want one: pick one or choose “Type your own prompt”.
  4. Print.

Three things to check

A sentence starts with a capital letter. There is a space the size of a finger between words. It stops with an end mark. After writing, your child points to each one and marks its box.

One good sentence

One sentence, said out loud first and then written with care, is plenty for a beginner. More lines are there for the days when there is more to say.

Good questions

What is on the checklist?

Three boxes: a capital letter, finger spaces, an end mark.

Can I print it without the picture box?

Yes. Choose “No box” and the lines fill the page.

Can I add a prompt?

Yes. Pick one from the list or type your own. It is printed in a small box for the grown-up to read out loud.

Is it free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no limit.
