How a day goes

One sheet a day. You say how it went, and the next sheet is made from what you said.

  1. PrintOpen your family page. Today’s sheet is waiting.
  2. Sit togetherA few minutes of your time at the start.
  3. Say how it wentEasy, Just right or Hard, and a note in your own words.
  4. Your sheet is madeOn the spot, with three lines that tell you what is on it and why.

After a hard day, the next sheet is an easier one, with nothing new on it. When that goes well, your child goes on from where they were.

Questions

What do I need?

A printer, a pencil for your child, and about five minutes. There is nothing to install. Your child should be able to read short sentences, or you can read them aloud.

How is a sheet made for my child?

You say how the sheet went and what you saw. The Sheet Maker builds the next sheet around exactly that, out of sentences from the stage your child is on or an earlier one, and it is ready in seconds. When a day goes well, the next sheet moves on. The answers are not printed on your child’s sheet. They are on your family page.

What can I write in the note?

Plain words. For example: the capital letter or the period that was forgotten; is and are mixed up; the s left off for more than one; a letter written backward, like b or d; or that it was a tired day. A rule your child has not reached yet waits until the road gets there. If the note is about something else, your choice of Easy, Just right or Hard still decides the next sheet.

What if my child already knows some grammar?

Then the starting check places your child further along the road, and the sheets begin there. You do not have to start from capital letters.

How long does a sheet take?

A few minutes of your time at the start. Children work at different speeds, so we do not put a number on your child’s part.

How do I cancel?

Any time. Open your orders page at Polar, the shop that handles our payments, and cancel there.

Is my child’s information safe?

The family page stores a nickname and the grammar record. Nothing else. Nothing watches, listens to or records your child.

Is there reading and math too?

Yes. Reading and math are their own plans, made the same way, at $9.99 a month each. Or take all three together for $14.95 a month. One family page holds them all.
