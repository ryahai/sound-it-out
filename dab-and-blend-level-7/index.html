Who it is for

Kindergarten and 1st grade children who are starting to join sounds into words, usually ages 5 to 7. They should know these sounds: s a t p i n m d g o c k ck e u r h b f ff l ll ss j v w x y z zz qu. Good for home, homeschool, reading centers and small groups.

Good questions

Do I need dot markers?

No. A crayon, a sticker or a fingertip dipped in paint works just as well.

What exactly do I download?

The same 13 pages as PDF files in full color and in ink-saving black and white, each sized for US Letter paper and for A4. Print the one you like.

How do I get it?

The button takes you to the download page, run by Polar. There is nothing to pay. It asks for your email address so it can send you the files.

Can I use it with my class?

Yes. Print as often as you like for your own children or your own class.
